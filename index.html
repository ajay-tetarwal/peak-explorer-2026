Our purpose

A thoughtful archive for the mountains.

Mountain Explorer brings mountain history, first ascents, expedition routes, camps, climbing seasons and summit records into one carefully organised knowledge base. The goal is to preserve reliable information and give climbers a way to improve it with sourced contributions.

Historical facts in this demo are sample catalogue entries and should be independently verified before public launch. User submissions need moderation and source citations.

How the archive works

Each mountain has a separate page. Community members can suggest updates; editors review sources, accuracy and safety context before publishing. A connected backend will make approved updates visible across devices without rebuilding the app.
